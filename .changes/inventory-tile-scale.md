Inventory tiles draw their specimen one way: a specimen that fills a phone screen's width (a row, card, banner) is laid out on the 402px reference and scaled to fit, so a part tile and a page tile of the same kind of thing come out at the same scale, and a fixed-size specimen (avatar, badge, dot, icon) is drawn at real size; a group whose tiles are all `fixedSize` uses narrower tiles and up to six columns. A thumbnail now draws a page's first specimen the way the page does: one the page draws filled (`fill`/`itemsFill`) stretches to the cell instead of collapsing to nothing when its root has no width of its own; a part tile can ask for the same with `InventoryTile`'s new `fill`. A filled specimen is capped at a phone's content width and centred, as on its page (in a cell no wider than a phone), and a narrow fixed-width root keeps its own size; a `specimenSize: 'page'` page counts as filled; a `render()` preview is laid out in a stretched column; a transparent surface stays transparent. Real-size drawing of fixed-size specimens is measured on web only. `ThumbnailSource` gains `fill` and `uncapped`, and `InventoryFixedSize` is exported.
