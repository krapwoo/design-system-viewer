A category overview's numbers are now its filters: the new `Inventory` wraps `InventoryGroup`s and draws one chip per group and per `InventoryTile` `tags` set (plus "All N"), each with its count, that shows exactly those tiles (a group wrapped in your own component is drawn in full and not counted; groups with the same title share a chip); a page can set `pageKind: 'style'` to show a "Style" badge beside its title (a button that opens a popover explaining what a style is) and on its inventory tile, and a style page is never suggested as a part of another page (`doctor`, `partGroups`, the Parts block), while naming one in `composedOf` still works and reads "· style"; `doctor --render` reports `grid-unused-width` for fixed-width tiles that wrap and strand space; and spacing is tightened: a banner keeps 16px below itself (no margin above, so it no longer adds to a parent's gap or padding above it; below, the 16px still adds to a following gap), the update banner uses the same 16px, a token's "Details ›" sits 4px under its tile with its tap target hanging below the text instead of over the line above, `TokenRow` columns sit together instead of spreading across the card (a long note stays beside its sample, 60 characters wide at most), and a `MotionSpecimen` transition stage stretches to its tile (up to 480px, 200px alone whatever its caption says). The space between a token page's jump row and its first section is unchanged. `TRANSITION_STAGE_MAX_WIDTH`, `StyleBadgeButton`, `STYLE_BADGE_LABEL` and `Popover` are exported, and `Inventory` takes a `layout` prop (`'sections'` or `'inline'`).
- The inventory's "Showing N of M" line is shown only while a filter narrows the list; under All it stays mounted but visually hidden, so the next change is still announced.
- `part-own-page` no longer asks for a style page to become a part.
- An `InventoryTile` that links to a member of a style page shows the Style badge too.
- Anatomy pins no longer crowd the specimen or each other: the pin lane starts 12px off the specimen's edge (`CATALOG_SPACE.md`; the picture frame's side margin grows from 30px to 60px), pins whose targets are closer than a pin's height are spread along the lane with slanted leader lines back to their targets, and a bracket you mark `testID="anatomy-bracket"` keeps 8px from every pin. A spread crowd stays inside the specimen's height, a pin's ↗ badge counts in its room, and in right-to-left text the pins spread without leader lines. `doctor --render` reports `anatomy-pin-crowded` for every `AnatomyPin` in a picture, in the `pins` lane or placed by hand in the picture's own layout: pins that overlap or touch (↗ badges included), a hand-placed pin that overlaps or touches text, a control or a bracket (marked or hand-drawn), a lane pin or bracket under 12px from the specimen, or a lane pin on its bracket. Keep hand-placed pins 4px apart and 8px from content; prefer the `pins` lane.
- A picture now needs more room to sit beside its legend: half the box must be at least the preview width + 168px (was + 108px, the margin grew 30→60px on each side), so a picture at a 360px preview width, such as the starter kit's TripCard at a 1280px window, now goes below its legend instead of beside it.
- The Style badge on a page head is a button named "Style — what this means" with a 44px hit area around the pill. Pressing it opens a popover anchored to the badge: the page's `styleNote` first when it has one, then what a style is, how it differs from a component and how to read the page, in short paragraphs, at most 320px wide. It opens below the badge, flips above near the bottom of the window, shifts sideways near an edge and never overflows a narrow window; a second press, Escape, a press outside or scrolling closes it; focus moves into it and returns to the badge; it is a non-modal `role="dialog"` named "About styles", one at a time, with an opacity fade that respects reduced motion. The badge is centred on the title's first line and no longer drops under a long title. The badge on an inventory tile stays decorative.
- An `Inventory` follows the token-page layout. A page whose `render` is an `Inventory` and nothing else (directly, or in a fragment under `WarningBanner`s), whatever the page's `previewWidths` (never in phone frames, no "Preview" label), is drawn at page level: the banner, then the filter chips in the same row and place as a token page's pills, then each `InventoryGroup` as its own section (uppercase title and description outside, tiles in the group's own bordered box), built from the same section frame and chip row a token page uses. A filter that leaves a group empty hides its whole section. This is the default, so existing overview pages that are an `Inventory` change without an edit; an `Inventory` inside a section or an example stays one inline box, and `layout="sections"` / `layout="inline"` overrides either way.
- `doctor --render` reports `sections-in-one-box` (warning) for a token page's parts drawn into one box: an `Inventory`'s chips, or a banner with groups, or two or more groups in one box, and a token gallery (`tokenGallery` with a `render`) that draws two or more titled groups in its one box (use `tokenSections`). The starter kit's Colors, Font and Icons pages now use `tokenSections`, so each kind of token is its own box.
- `composedOf` has a new relationship, `'arranged'`: a separate component this one lays out alongside others in its region (what makes a page a pattern). It counts as drawn by the page, like `'built-in'`: it is a Part (numbered, a card, in the anatomy legend) and feeds "Also used by"; the Composition list shows it as "· arranged". `part-own-page` ignores it. A component built from other components stays one component (`'built-in'`).
- `doctor` checks page placement (`page-placement`, warning), opt-in with `doctor: { placement: true }` and `partGroups`: a page in a part group whose `composedOf` (page or any member) has an `'arranged'` entry naming a catalogued component is a pattern (move it to the first `patternGroups` entry); a page in a pattern group whose `composedOf` was read in full and has no `'arranged'` entry is a component (move it to the first part group that is not also a pattern or screen group); a page that declares any `'arranged'` entry is never told it arranges nothing; a group page whose members disagree lists which are which and recommends by majority or a split. Rendered JSX never decides (it can't tell built-from from arranged); a message may name it as context. Style, token, reference and documentation-only pages and members and anything not catalogued are never counted; an `arranged` entry may name one of the page's own members, and a component listed in another page's `documents` is catalogued; the component groups are `partGroups` less the pattern and screen groups, so a project that lists patterns among its parts keeps that setting; pages in the new `screenGroups` (default `['Screens']`) are exempt, and a page whose `composedOf` can't be read is never reported. With `placement: true`, loading the config warns when no component group remains, when `placement` isn't a boolean, or when `patternGroups` is empty.
- A parent with exactly one child (`sidebarParent`) adds a level for nothing: the sidebar now shows that child flat, right after its parent, and `doctor` warns `single-child-parent`, naming both.
