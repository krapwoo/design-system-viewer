The starter kit's `Button` now gives small buttons a 4pt vertical hit slop to reach the 44pt touch target, `Shimmer` and `Breathing` render a static block when the OS Reduce Motion setting is on (new `useReduceMotion` hook; `Shimmer` also takes an `accessibilityLabel`), `EmptyState` marks its title as a heading and hides its icon from screen readers, and a selected `Pill` now announces its label and selected state.
