Three fixes to the readability check, member titles and the sidebar:
- The `specimen-text-illegible` check no longer flags white text on a deliberately coloured fill (such as a white checkmark on a yellow swatch); text on a grey or tinted-grey background still counts as drawn for the other appearance.
- A family or group page drawn as an example inside another catalog now shows its own members' titles instead of ids or the outer catalog's titles.
- Clearing the sidebar filter now scrolls to the open page's row if you left that page and came back while the filter hid its row.
